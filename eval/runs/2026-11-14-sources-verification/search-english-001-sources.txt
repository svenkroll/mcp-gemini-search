  According to **RFC 1035**, the maximum length of a single DNS label is **63 octets** (or less).   ### Primary Source of Truth * **Publisher of Record / Standards Body:** RFC Editor / Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF) * **Specification:** [RFC 1035: *Domain Names - Implementation and Specification*](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc1035)   * **Section 2.3.4 ("Size limits"):** Explicitly lists `labels 63 octets or less`.   * **Section 3.1 ("Name space definitions"):** Notes that *"The high order two bits of every length octet must be zero, and the remaining six bits of the length field limit the label to 63 octets or less."*
