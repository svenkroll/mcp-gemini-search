  According to the official Kubernetes documentation on **Pod Lifecycle**, the default value of a Pod's `restartPolicy` field is **`Always`**.  The `spec` of a Pod allows values of `Always`, `OnFailure`, and `Never`.  ### Primary Source of Truth: * **Source:** Kubernetes Documentation — *Pod Lifecycle* ("Pod-level container restart policy" section) * **URL:** [Kubernetes: Pod Lifecycle – Restart Policy](https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/workloads/pods/pod-lifecycle/#restart-policy)
